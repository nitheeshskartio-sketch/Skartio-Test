Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Skartio Test ("Skartio Test", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users access our application and its Google Merchant Center integration features.

1. About Skartio Test

Skartio Test is a demonstration and integration-testing application designed to connect the Skartio platform with Google Merchant Center. It enables authorized users to test product catalogue data synchronization and related Merchant Center operations.

2. Information we access

Depending on the features used and the permissions granted by the user, the application may access or process the following information:

  • Google account authorization information: authorization credentials or tokens required to maintain the connection that the user has approved.
  • Merchant Center account information: account identifiers and related account details needed to identify the Merchant Center account selected for integration.
  • Product information: catalogue attributes such as product IDs, titles, descriptions, links, images, prices, availability, condition, brand, GTIN, and other attributes made available through the enabled Merchant API operations.
  • Integration information: synchronization status, timestamps, request outcomes, and technical error details needed to operate and troubleshoot the integration.
  • Technical information: basic server and access logs that may include IP address, browser details, timestamps, and diagnostic information.

The exact information accessed depends on the Google permissions granted and the Merchant API operations enabled for the integration.

3. How we use information

We use information accessed through the application for the following purposes:

  • Authenticate and maintain a user-authorized connection to Google services.
  • Identify the Merchant Center account selected by the authorized user.
  • Read product and catalogue information needed for synchronization and validation.
  • Submit or update product information in Merchant Center when the user or configured integration workflow requests it and the granted permissions allow it.
  • Display synchronization status and help users investigate failed or incomplete operations.
  • Protect, maintain, and improve the reliability and security of the integration.

4. Google user data and limited use

Skartio Test's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Google data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features of the application that are relevant to the requested Merchant Center integration. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising, personalized advertising, or unrelated purposes.

We do not permit humans to read Google user data except where the user has provided affirmative consent, where it is necessary for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or where the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations in accordance with Google's requirements.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent Google user data. Information may be transmitted to Google services as necessary to perform the Merchant Center operations requested by the authorized user. We may use infrastructure or service providers to host and operate the application, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. We do not share Google user data with third parties for their independent advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Data storage and retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the integration, maintain synchronization records, meet legitimate operational requirements, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Authorization credentials, where stored, are intended to be protected using appropriate access controls and security measures.

Retention periods may vary according to the type of information and the configuration of the integration. Users may request disconnection of the Google account and deletion of associated integration data, subject to applicable legal or operational retention requirements.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Revoking Google access

Users can revoke Skartio Test's access from their Google Account's third-party connections or security settings. Revoking access prevents the application from making further authorized requests to Google APIs. Some previously stored integration records may remain until deleted in accordance with our retention practices.

9. Children's privacy

Skartio Test is a business integration application and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact us

For questions, privacy requests, or concerns about this policy or the application's handling of information, contact the Skartio team through the official Skartio website or your existing Skartio support channel.
