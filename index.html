Skartio · Merchant Center integration

Manage your product data with Skartio Test

Skartio Test is a demonstration application designed to connect the Skartio platform with Google Merchant Center. It helps authorized users test the transfer and management of product catalogue information through Google's Merchant APIs.

What is Skartio Test?

Skartio Test is an integration utility for businesses and authorized administrators who want to test how product information managed in Skartio can be shared with Google Merchant Center. The application is intended for development, configuration, and integration validation.

This website describes the application and its data practices. The website itself does not provide a public product marketplace or accept customer orders.

What the application does

Connect Merchant Center

Allows an authorized user to connect a Google account and select or access the relevant Merchant Center account for the integration.

Product catalogue integration

Supports testing the transfer and synchronization of product information, such as product titles, descriptions, identifiers, prices, availability, and related catalogue attributes.

Sync monitoring

Helps authorized users validate integration activity and review the status of product data synchronization between Skartio and Google Merchant Center.

Why we request Google access

When an authorized user connects a Google account, Skartio Test requests the Google permissions needed to interact with the selected Merchant Center account. This access is used to perform the Merchant Center integration tasks initiated by that user, such as reading merchant account or product information and submitting product data for synchronization.

We use Google data only to provide and operate the integration features described above. We do not use Google account data to build advertising profiles, sell personal information, or serve unrelated advertising.

Access is user-authorized. The application can access Google Merchant Center information only after the account owner or an appropriately authorized user grants the requested permission.

Who can use this application?

Skartio Test is intended for Skartio administrators, developers, and business users who are authorized to configure or test a Merchant Center integration. It is not intended as a consumer shopping application.
